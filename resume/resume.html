Selected Research Projects

When Do Learned Diffusion Proposals Help Constraint Solving? A Controlled Study on Continuous Algebraic Systems — arXiv:2607.27169; under review at AAAI.
  • Compares graph-neural diffusion proposals against exact symbolic acceptance on continuous algebraic systems; reports a negative result under matched refinement budgets and a positive finding on learned repair ranking.
Belief-Space Perception Routing under Coupled Sensor Faults and Compute Contention — arXiv:2608.00322; under review at IEEE MIT URTC.
  • Models perception routing under belief uncertainty where sensor faults and compute contention are coupled rather than independent.
Substrate-Coevolving Neural Dynamics: A Diagnostic Study of Self-Modifying Computation — first author; SSRN:6892259.
  • Makes the computational interaction substrate an explicit state variable updated during the forward pass, then diagnoses the resulting failure modes, optimization barriers, and gated, latch-like substrate updates.
KAISER: Reproducible Subgroup Fairness Auditing for Clinical Risk Models — arXiv:2607.28608.
  • Fixes an auditing protocol so subgroup performance gaps in clinical risk models can be rerun and compared across studies rather than re-derived ad hoc.
MOSAIC — The Garden of Forking Tissues: A Specification-Curve Multiverse for Spatial Transcriptomics.
  • Sweeps a multiverse of defensible pipeline specifications and quantifies how much of a biological conclusion survives reasonable analytic choices.
Aura Farming the Good Life: Toward a Community-Based Epistemology of Learning in the Age of AI — open-science manuscript; in submission (Nature portfolio).
  • Argues for community-based epistemology as an alternative to credential-driven academic culture.
